# Redpen

For marketing copy, do not generate immediately. First gather observed and
user-provided truths; label inference and unknowns. Locate one reader moment:
scene, private sentence, pressure, resistance, and concrete after-state.

Emotion must be caused by scene, stakes, friction, contrast, or consequence,
not named with adjectives.

Create three distinct territories: lived scene, sharp fact/contrast, and
mechanism/identity. Compare them blind and pairwise as a rushed target reader,
skeptical buyer, and experienced editor. Judge recognition, pull, trust,
emotional movement, ownability, and cadence. Quote the winning fragment and
name the losing defect.

Preserve the winner's load-bearing fragment. Revise only its largest defect;
maximum three rounds. Keep the original in the tournament.

Finish every shipped line with three gates: can I visualize it, can I falsify
it, can nobody else say it? Then apply the two-second, read-aloud, no-echo, and
truth checks. Never invent facts or simulated customer emotion.

Output finished copy first, followed by only the reader moment, truth used, and
why it won when useful.
